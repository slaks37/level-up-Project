## 9. AI Architecture — Agnes AI 2.0 (Stage 9)

### 9.1 Core Architectural Principles
1. **Context-Aware Copilot, Never the Source of Truth:** Agnes AI 2.0 proposes breakdowns, questions, coaching drafts, and canvas hypotheses — **deterministic code on client/server remains 100% authoritative** for EXP, levels, competency status, quiz scoring, HPP/BEP/cash-flow math, permissions, streaks, and coin transactions.
2. **Explicit Assumption Tagging:** Every Business Studio suggestion generated by Agnes is stamped `origin: 'ai_assumption'` in UI and Firestore until the participant verifies it with real-world evidence (`origin: 'validated_evidence'`).
3. **Consent-Gated Data-Access Boundaries:** Private health metrics, moods, sleep logs, meal journals, and calendar titles are **NEVER sent to Agnes by default** unless the mode explicitly requires it AND the participant enabled the corresponding consent toggle in `users/{uid}/privacy/settings`.
4. **Structured Server-Side Prompts Only:** Eliminate raw client `messages[]` passthrough (already blocked in `proxy/gateway.mjs`, broken in `functions/src/index.ts`). The client sends `{ mode, action, scopedContextIds, userInput }`; the server fetches/validates authorized context, strips PII, builds the hardened system prompt, enforces per-mode token/rate limits, calls Agnes APIHub, and logs audit metadata (never logging sensitive journal contents into `aiUsageLogs`).

### 9.2 Ten Agnes AI 2.0 Expertise Modes & Data-Access Boundary Matrix

| # | Expertise Mode | Allowed Context (Default) | Restricted / Opt-In Only Context | Structured Output / App Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Personal Coach** | Purpose statement, Pathway, Dream Map milestones, streaks | Private journal/mood **ONLY if `sharePrivateJournalWithAgnes == true`** | Reflection prompts, habit obstacle review, micro-quest suggestions |
| 2 | **Career Coach** | Profession, Career profile (industry, role, project status, nextAction), Career tasks | Synced calendar titles **ONLY on explicit per-request opt-in** | Sprint/project action plan, meeting prep checklist, stakeholder scripts |
| 3 | **Learning Tutor** | Active course/module/lesson metadata, participant's quiz mistakes/rubric gaps | No health, finance, or private journal data | Socratic explanations, practice drills, study quests linked to lesson |
| 4 | **Business Mentor** | Active Venture canvases (BMC, VPC, Social Problem, Experiments) | No health/mood journal data | Hypothesis critique, experiment designs (`origin: 'ai_assumption'`), validation interview scripts |
| 5 | **Marketing Advisor** | Venture value proposition, target customer research, Marketing Plan canvas | No health/mood journal or personal finance | Positioning copy, channel test ideas, campaign experiment quests |
| 6 | **Financial Assistant** | Deterministic HPP, Pricing, Cash Flow, and BEP outputs (pre-computed by app!) | Never computes authoritative totals via LLM; no personal bank/health data | Plain-language margin diagnosis, cost-reduction hypotheses, pricing sensitivity prompts |
| 7 | **Wellness Companion** | Workout profile (`gym`), workout fatigue/pain flags, daily duration totals | Sleep, meals, energy, mood **ONLY if `shareHealthMetricsWithAgnes == true`**; strict non-medical disclaimer on pain | Gentle recovery framing, habit reminders (never medical diagnosis/prescriptions!) |
| 8 | **Export Assistant** | Venture Product Prototype, Legal Readiness & Export Readiness checklists | No health/mood journal data | HS-code prep checklists, packaging/compliance gap questions, B2B inquiry prep tasks |
| 9 | **Impact Advisor** | Social Problem Canvas, Community Potential Mapping, Social Impact metrics | No health/mood journal data | Theory-of-Change drafts, beneficiary interview guides, impact KPI definitions |
| 10 | **Daily Planner** | Pending quests, active Dream Step, Career next action, daily time budget | Calendar titles & energy status only if consented | Balanced Dual-Track daily schedule (Career + Dream + Health micro-blocks) |

### 9.3 Safety, Quota & Graceful Offline Degradation
- **Prompt Injection Defense:** All user text & canvas JSON are encapsulated as untrusted data blocks; output is strictly validated against per-mode JSON schemas (falling back cleanly to deterministic local engines in `coaching.ts` and `reflection.ts` if Agnes is offline, rate-limited, or returns malformed JSON).
- **Tiered Quotas:** Configurable in Admin (`appConfig/aiPublic`) — global daily cap + per-mode token budgets (e.g., `max_tokens: 1200` for coaching, `2200` for roadmap/canvas drafts) + atomic server quota reservation via Firestore transaction.
- **PII Redaction:** Server pre-processor strips email addresses, phone numbers, and Indonesian NIK patterns before outbound calls to Agnes APIHub.

## 10. Gamification Architecture — 3-Axis Progression (Stage 6)

LevelUp 2.0 **preserves** the beloved RPG feel (EXP, Levels, Career Ranks `E → C → A → S`, 5 Scientific Stats, Daily/Task/Workout Streaks, 1-Coin Economy) while solving gamification's #1 flaw: **never equating hours logged or EXP farmed with real-world mastery or business viability.**

### 10.1 Three Strictly Separated Progression Axes

| Axis | Name | What It Measures | How It Advances (Authoritative Rule) | What It Does NOT Grant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Axis 1** | **Experience Level** *(Preserved)* | Consistency, effort, deliberate practice hours, physical vitality | Daily quests, check-ins, workouts, streaks, learning time (`EXP` + `STR/VIT/INT/AGI/CHA` + Career Rank `E→S`). | **NEVER** grants Competency Levels, Certificates, or Business Levels! |
| **Axis 2** | **Competency Level** (`C1–C10`) *(New)* | Proven mastery of specific skills & knowledge | Deterministic quiz mastery (`>=80%`) + practical assignment submission + **verified evidence / mentor rubric approval** (`users/{uid}/competencies`). | Cannot be farmed via repeating easy quests or watching videos passively. |
| **Axis 3** | **Business Development Level** (`B0–B10`) *(New)* | Real-world venture maturity & validated social/market traction | Evidence-gated Business Studio milestones: `B1` Problem Validated (real interviews) → `B2` Value Prop Tested → `B3` Prototype Built → `B4` Pilot/First Sale Proof → `B5` Unit Economics Positive → `B6` Legal Ready → `B7` Marketplace Listed (`sociopreneur.online`) → `B8` Repeat Revenue → `B9` Export/Scale Ready → `B10` Verified Social Impact. | Cannot advance via AI-generated canvases alone (`ai_assumption` blocks stage promotion until `validated_evidence` exists!). |

### 10.2 Visual HUD Integration
- `StatusPanel` shows the familiar **Experience Level + Career Rank** hero badge alongside two compact progress pills: **`COMPETENCY: C2`** and **`BUSINESS: B3 (Prototype)`** (Business pill displays cleanly when user selects an entrepreneurial/sociopreneur/business pathway or creates a venture).
- **Skill Arsenal** visually distinguishes:
  - *Habit Skills* (`Rutin Terlatih` — unlocked via 3 sessions / 120 mins, preserved from v1)
  - *Verified Competencies* (`✓ Terverifikasi` — unlocked via Academy + Evidence/Mentor review, portable to Certificates & Portfolio).

## 11. LMS Architecture — LevelUp Academy (Stage 7)

LevelUp Academy is a **native module inside LevelUp OS** — learning never sits in an isolated video library; every module injects actionable tasks into the user's **Dream Map**, **Daily Quests**, and **Business Studio**.

### 11.1 Core LMS Capabilities
1. **Course Catalog & Access Control:**
   - Supports 3 access types: `free` (open to all), `cohort_only` (assigned via Admin/Institution), and `paid_external` (unlocked exclusively via verified `sociopreneur.online` purchase webhook → `enrollments/{uid}_{courseId}`).
   - Filterable by Pathway, Development Dimension, and Target Competency.
2. **Course Authoring & Management (Staff Console — `trainer` / `admin`):**
   - Create/edit Courses, Modules, Lessons (`article`, `video` via safe allowlisted embeds/hosted stream, `quiz`, `assignment`, `studio_action`), Competency mappings, and Rubric templates.
   - Versioned publishing (`draft` → `published` → `archived`) so editing live lessons never breaks active cohorts.
3. **Interactive Assessments & Server-Graded Quizzes:**
   - Quiz answer keys live in server-only `quizKeys/{lessonId}` (unreadable by clients); grading runs server-side, returning itemized feedback and deterministic pass/fail (`score >= passingScore`).
   - Practical assignments link directly to `users/{uid}/evidence/{evidenceId}` or a **Business Studio Canvas snapshot**.
4. **Cohorts & Mentor Review Queue:**
   - Cohorts group learners with assigned `mentorUids` and `trainerUids` and optional weekly pacing milestones.
   - Mentors get a dedicated **Review Queue** (powered by composite index `(cohortId, status, submittedAt)`) with rubric sliders, structured feedback, `Approve` / `Request Revision` actions, and zero access to learners' private health/mood journals.
5. **Portfolio Evidence & Verifiable Certificates:**
   - Completing all required modules + passing quizzes + receiving mentor approval on required practical evidence triggers **server-side Certificate issuance** (`certificates/{certificateCode}`) with a public verification URL (`/verify/{certificateCode}`), SHA-256 verification hash, linked competency list, and PDF export.
6. **Bidirectional Gamified OS Loops:**
   - **Academy → Daily Quests:** Enrolling in a course or starting a lesson offers 1-click *"Masukkan ke Tugas Harian"* (or auto-schedules via Daily Planner) tagged with `linkedLessonId` & `dimension`.
   - **Academy → Dream Map:** Pathway courses map directly to Dream Map stages (e.g., Sociopreneur Stage 4 `Learn` and Stage 5 `Design`).
   - **Academy → Business Studio:** `studio_action` lessons open the exact Business Studio canvas (e.g., Lesson *"Menghitung HPP Produk Pangan"* opens the **HPP & Pricing Calculator** pre-linked to the assignment!).
   - **Academy → Skill Tree:** Passing competency assessments promotes the corresponding skill in Skill Arsenal to **Verified Competency**.

## 12. Business Studio Architecture (Stage 8)

An interactive, offline-capable venture workspace organized into **4 Studio Wings** covering all **16 Required Tools**. Each participant can manage 1 or more ventures (`users/{uid}/ventures/{ventureId}`).

### 12.2 Strict Assumption vs Evidence Visual Contract
Every Business Studio card displays a persistent trust badge:
- 🟡 **Asumsi AI (`ai_assumption`)** — Drafted with Agnes AI; useful for brainstorming, **0 credit** toward Business Level advancement.
- 🔵 **Catatan Draf (`user_input`)** — Written by the founder; ready to be tested in the field.
- 🟢 **Tervalidasi Lapangan (`validated_evidence`)** — Linked to logged interviews, experiment results, financial proof, or mentor-approved `EvidenceRecord`; counts directly toward **Business Development Level** and **`sociopreneur.online` Product Readiness**!
